# Aged-care worker requirements — advisory checklist (Australia, home and community care)

## Scope — check this before using the list

This checklist covers **in-home and community aged care**: Support at Home, Home Care
Packages, and Commonwealth Home Support Programme services. It deliberately leaves out
requirements that apply only to residential aged care, such as the residential
mandatory flu vaccination program.

**Supported Independent Living (SIL) is an NDIS support, not aged care.** A provider
asking about SIL wants the NDIS check in `references/ndis-worker-requirements.md`. If
the user's account mixes SIL and home care — common in Australia — say so and offer to
run both checks, reporting each staff member against the framework that matches the
work they actually do.

**The two sectors overlap on screening.** A worker who holds a current NDIS Worker
Screening Check may use it in place of an aged-care police certificate. So a SIL and
home-care provider should not be reported as missing an aged-care police check for a
worker whose NDIS check is current. Say this explicitly when the account holds NDIS
screening records.

## Read this caveat out to the user before reporting against this list

> This is a general checklist of worker requirements commonly expected of Australian
> home care and community aged care providers. Aged-care worker screening aligned to the
> NDIS worker check applies, and existing police certificates remain usable until they
> expire. Vaccination requirements for home care sit with provider policy and with state
> and territory health departments rather than a federal mandate. Several items below are
> sector norms rather than law. Confirm current requirements against the [Aged Care
> Quality and Safety Commission](https://www.agedcarequality.gov.au) and the [Department
> of Health, Disability and Ageing](https://www.health.gov.au) before relying on this. It
> is not legal advice and it is not an audit outcome.

Quote that caveat, do not paraphrase it. Report against this list only when the user
has explicitly chosen the aged-care check.

## Role-specific credentials

Certificate III is a widely-held sector expectation for personal care work, not a
legislated minimum for home care. Say so rather than reporting it as a breach.
